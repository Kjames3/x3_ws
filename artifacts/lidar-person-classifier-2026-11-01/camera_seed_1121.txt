Method.  For each measured C3 camera person (lidar_persons.ndjson camera[], as
lidar_person_dataset.camera_people) the body is searched along the camera's LINE OF SIGHT
(corridor +-0.35 m, 1.2 m nearer to 0.5 m beyond the camera range), not at the camera XY:
points above 0.85 m (desk tops are ~0.75) are cut into objects along the ray (gap 0.2 m) and
the NEAREST one with >= 8 points is the body; the candidate is every point 0.1-2.0 m high
within 0.3 m (XY) of its centre and 0.3 m of its range along the ray (drops the desk and wall
behind), with extract's size tests.  Deduplicated against extract's candidates within 0.5 m.
No image-box restriction: lidar_persons.ndjson carries no boxes (c3/c3.ndjson does, but in
monotonic time), so this is the plain-XY version.

WHY THE RAY, NOT THE XY (found while building this): on the 11-20 lab runs the C3 range
over-reads by ~0.8 m.  EVERY C3 box in seated-r1/r2/r3 and walk-r1/r2 extends above the image
(bbox y1 ~ -116; 100% of 995 measurements; apartment runs 0-32%, apt-seated-desk-r1 93%).
person_measurement's torso window (20-55% of the box height) then clips to image rows 0-60,
which show the partition behind the user (depth 2.7-3.7 m) while the rest of the box reads
~2.4 m.  Recomputing with the box clipped to the image first moves the seated range nearer by
0.37 / 0.48 m (median, seated-r1 / r2): about half of the 0.78 / 0.83 m body->camera offset.

Ground truth.  Seeded candidates sit on a camera person by construction, so the auto-labeller
would always call them people; here they are labelled only by fixed-XY overrides (no
near_camera ones) or as clutter in empty-r1.  Base rows keep lidar_person_dataset's labels.
Controls seed every fixed-XY override seat on every frame of its window, no dedup.

FINDINGS
1. Apartment seated (the extractor-merge case): fixed.  Camera persons with a candidate within
   0.6 m: chair/couch 7-10% -> 99-100%, scored > 0.5 76-97%; apt-desk2 park30 33 -> 100%,
   drive-r2 28 -> 98% (94% > 0.5).  New rows land 0.14-0.20 m (median) from the camera XY, inside
   the tracker's 0.55 m camera gate.  Exception apt-seated-desk-r1 (seen from behind over the
   left shoulder): found 98%, but the classifier scores only 15% > 0.5.
2. Lab desk (seated-r1/r2): NOT an extractor problem.  On 345/552 and 397/576 instances a BASE
   candidate already sits on the body found along the ray, 93-100% of them > 0.5; the camera XY
   is 0.78-0.83 m behind it (finding above), so the 0.6 m metric (8.5/11.3%) and the live 0.55 m
   camera gate both miss.  Seeding finds a body on 100% of instances (81/91% > 0.5) but only
   adds a new row on 31-37%, and within 0.6 m of the camera XY it reaches just 16-18%.  Fix
   the C3 range (clip the box to the image before the torso window; or take the nearest depth
   mode) and/or associate camera <-> lidar along the bearing; seeding alone does not fix this.
3. Clutter.  Seeded rows exist only at camera detections: +0.07 (standing), +0.28 (seated),
   +0.09 (empty) per frame, vs base 4.1 / 0.5 / 1.0.  Labelled seeded clutter scored > 0.5:
   0.003 per frame in the empty runs (base 0.021) - but those are 12/12 of the labelled
   bedroom camera false positives in apt-desk2-empty-drive-r1: the lidar shape does NOT veto
   a camera false positive on desk furniture.  empty-r1 has no camera detections at all, so
   the lab gives no camera-FP test.  Controls: empty chairs at known positions give NO body
   (0/746 frames, apt-desk2 roommate chair); bed/cabinet a body on 63-100% of frames but
   0.1-31% > 0.5; the two scan-matched empty chairs (+-0.6 m uncertain) 22% / 64% > 0.5;
   occupied seats 59-100% > 0.5 (desk-r1 11%).
4. Standing/walking never drop (seeded rows only add; base rows unchanged).  Crowds gain:
   group-r1 75 -> 99% within 0.6 m (> 0.5: 75 -> 96%), busy-r2 81 -> 91%, busy-r1 64 -> 66%.
   Those added rows are camera-confirmed but unlabelled.
5. Runtime (laptop, 27 processes): 0.42 ms median / 0.85 ms p95 per frame with seeds, vs
   extract 1.9 / 5.2 ms.  Expect ~2-4x on the Orin; still small.

CAVEATS: model_base_1121 was trained on these runs (scores in-sample; seated rows include the
override-labelled seated pieces).  Unlabelled seeded rows are judged only through the camera.
seated-r3 has 15 camera instances; apt-desk2-empty-park15-r1 is not empty (user seated out of
view, roommate 20-40 s).  Parameters were set on two seated-r1 probes and never tuned.

VERDICT: Worth a live SHADOW test (publish seeded rows on a separate topic, tracker unchanged),
mainly for seated people at home and close groups, where it turns 7-30% into ~99%
coverage at ~0.5 ms and +0.1-0.3 candidates/frame, with no standing-person loss.  It does NOT fix the lab
desk case on its own: there the extractor already has the user and C3's top-clipped boxes put
the camera 0.8 m behind them, so fix the C3 range first and re-run this script.  Also check
camera false positives on furniture in the lab before letting seeded rows birth tracks:
the classifier passes them (12/12 in the apartment bedroom).
