== Reading the numbers (written by hand after the run; the sections above are script output) ==
Reproduced baseline: arm a, generator/fit seed 0, is AP 0.904 overall (same as results_base_1121.txt). The HGB fit seed alone moves it
0.892-0.904, so differences under ~0.01 overall are noise.

1. Synthetic data does not help the classifier on the real runs, existing or new.
   all-runs AP: a 0.897 | b (existing, 2.2k rows) 0.869 | b_big (4.4k rows) 0.850 | c_all (new + existing, 8.7k rows) 0.831.
   Down-weighting every synthetic row to 0.25 (b_w25 0.880, c_w25 0.853) and dropping the existing rows (c_new 0.864, c_pos 0.872)
   shrink the loss but never turn it positive. The synthetic-only model (d) scores 0.50 on real runs and 0.2-1.8 false alarms
   per frame on the person-free runs.
2. Seated runs: no headroom to win. The real seated candidates the extractor already produces are classified at AP 0.98 and
   recall 0.98 with real data only; adding synthetic content moves the pooled seated AP by -0.010 [-0.052, +0.014] (c_all - a)
   and by -0.010 [-0.052, +0.013] (c_all - b_big): no effect inside the run-bootstrap noise. Exploratory subsets (not in the decision rule):
   apt chair/couch/desk +0.021 [-0.004, +0.052], lab desk 11-20 -0.037 [-0.090, 0.000], furniture-r1 + goal-on-r4 -0.088 [-0.142, -0.013].
3. Where it hurts: furniture-r1. Its real seated person is one 1139-point blob at 0.69 m, 0.71 m tall (z_hi 1.0 m). The synthetic
   empty chairs/desks it is shown as negatives at < 1.5 m (median height 0.94, n 929) look more like it than the synthetic seated
   people do (height 1.08, z_hi 1.27). Seated recall on that run goes 1.00 -> 0.07 with c_all; c_pos (positives only) leaves it
   at 0.98 and c_neg (negatives only) reproduces the loss (0.31 over the pair of runs), so the furniture negatives cause it.
4. Crowd runs lose with every synthetic arm (a 0.727; b 0.679, b_big 0.645, c_all 0.636; CI wide, the three runs are one lab), close
   runs are flat (+-0.002).
5. Fidelity gap, measured two ways. (i) A classifier separates synthetic from real person candidates at AUC 0.976-0.997
   (0-4 m, standing and seated); two random halves of the real runs give 0.42. No single feature does it (best single-feature AUC
   0.70, band_2 / z_hi); it is the joint shape. Real standing people carry ~40% more points than the ellipsoid bodies
   (area 11.7 vs 9.1), wider legs (0.32 vs 0.26 m). (ii) Median-feature gaps > 10% in all three range bands (depth +18..+32%,
   top_fill +43% at 0-2 m, n +21%, width +38% at 4-6 m, only 30 real label-2 candidates there).
6. Extractor side (what step 0 was asked to show about merging). With furniture, the extractor yields a candidate that is within
   0.35 m of the synthetic seated person for 50% (desk), 66% (chair), 29% (couch) of scored people; the same people with the furniture
   removed give 80%, 76%, 59%: furniture costs 10-30 points, mostly as MISSED (no candidate covers them), not as a merge
   with an off-centre centre (found vs "cov" differ by 1-3 points, except couch at 0.4-1.5 m where 35% found / 70% cov).
   Standing people beside furniture: 75% found vs 96% bare; a partition beside them costs the most (59% vs 97%), a couch the least.
   Back view at a desk, the usual real case: 56% found overall, 67% at 0.4-1.5 m (bare: 92% at 0.4-1.5 m, 80% overall).
   Compared with what the repo measured on real runs (memory notes, different metric and runs, so indicative only): apt-desk2
   back-view seated people 0.95-1.0 recall, apartment chair/couch ~5-10% of frames, lab desk 47 of 65 seated candidates.
   The synthetic desk rate is well below the real desk rate and the synthetic chair/couch rate is well above the real one: the
   extractor numbers from this generator are not calibrated to either, so they cannot yet steer extractor changes.
7. Limits: z_hi/top_fill etc. use the 0.31 m sensor height for every background frame, the v2 chassis (11-20 lab runs) is
   0.302 m; no robot ego-motion; furniture is solid boxes; synthetic negatives exist only for furniture (the real runs supply all
   other negatives), so synthetic positives are never matched by synthetic negatives of the same domain except near furniture;
   one generator design, 3 seeds; subset results rest on few runs.
